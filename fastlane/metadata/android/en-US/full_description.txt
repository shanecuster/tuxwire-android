tuxwire is an RSS reader that looks and feels like a terminal. Follow the blogs and sites you care about in one calm, distraction-free place: monospace text, bordered panels and colour themes, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Why you will like it
- Stay focused. Every feed is plain text in a clean layout, so you read the words and nothing else.
- Keep what matters. Save any article and attach your own note to it, then export everything to one Markdown file.
- Stay in control. Your feeds, saved articles and notes live on your phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.
- Use it with one hand. Pinned top and bottom bars keep every control within reach of your thumb.

Feeds and reading
- Add a feed by its address, or by a blog's address: tuxwire finds the feed for you.
- Browse your feeds by topic, then articles, then a plain-text summary of each one.
- A * beside a feed shows it got new articles after a refresh.
- Open the full article in your browser whenever you want the whole page.
- Import and export your feed list as OPML, the format other RSS readers use, so moving in or out takes a minute.
- Some sites block apps. For those, import the feed from a file you saved, and tuxwire tells you clearly when a site refuses.

Read later
- Keep a list of links to open later: paste one in, or share it from your browser.
- Opened links stay on the list, drawn like read articles, until you drop them or save them. It always asks first.
- tuxwire reads only the page's title so a link has a name. Nothing else is fetched from the page.

Share from your browser
- Use Android's Share menu to send a page to tuxwire, then choose each time: read it later, save it, or add its feed.

Saved articles and notes
- Save an article and write a note on it: why it mattered, what to follow up on, a quote you liked.
- Export every saved article and note to a single Markdown file through Android's own file picker, ready for your notes app.
- Un-saving, deleting a note and anything else that removes something always asks first.

Know your reading habits
- The Stats tab shows articles read, your current and best streak, reads per week, and the topics and sources you read most.

Optional sync to your own server
- Sync is off until you turn it on. It copies your saved articles, notes and Read Later links to a CouchDB server that you run yourself, and only when you tap "sync now".
- Separate switches, all off by default, can also copy your feed list and which articles you have opened, so everything matches across your devices.
- The server address must use https, and the password is stored encrypted on the phone using the Android Keystore.

Private by design
- The only permission is internet access, to download your feeds.
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
- The app opts out of cloud backups. Uninstalling removes your data.
- Read the full privacy policy from the About tab.

Free and open source
- tuxwire is free software under the MIT licence. The Rust engine is shared with the tuxwire terminal app, so both behave the same way.
- Share tuxwire with a friend or rate it on Google Play from the About tab.

If you want a minimal, ad-free RSS reader with a terminal look, tuxwire is for you.
