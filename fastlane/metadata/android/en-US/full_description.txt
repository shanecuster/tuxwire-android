tuxwire is an RSS reader that looks and feels like a terminal: monospace text, bordered panels, and colour themes shared with the tuxwire terminal app.

Feeds and reading
- Add a feed by its address or by a blog's address; tuxwire finds the feed for you.
- Browse feeds by topic, then articles, then a plain-text summary of each one.
- Open the full article in your browser when you want it.
- Share a page from your browser to tuxwire to add its feed.
- Import and export your feed list as OPML, the format other feed readers use.
- Pinned top and bottom bars keep everything within reach of one hand.

Saved articles and notes
- Save any article and attach a note to it.
- Un-saving, deleting a note, and anything else that removes something always asks first.
- Export every saved article and note to one Markdown file, through Android's own file picker.

Optional sync to your own server
- Sync is off until you turn it on. It copies only your saved articles and notes to a CouchDB server that you run yourself, and only when you tap "sync now".
- The server address must use https, and the password is stored encrypted on the phone using the Android Keystore.

Privacy
- The only permission is internet access, to download feeds.
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on the phone unless you set up your own sync server.
- The app opts out of cloud backups.
